# Scorecard policy

Sales contains the original 17 checkpoints. Gold: 17; Green: 14–16; Below: 0–13. General and Retention each contain their own 12 checkpoints. Gold: 12; Green: 11; Below: 0–10. Exact IDs, labels and guidance live in `src/lib/scoring/rubrics.ts`.

Passed earns one point; missed earns zero. A complete Sales call with reliably no objections earns exactly four objection-section policy points. Unknown, unverifiable or unresolved applicability does not change the denominator or become a pass. Incomplete calls, unresolved checkpoints or any independent review reason withhold the official grade. Missing/duplicate/wrong rubric checkpoints are rejected.

Evidence quotes must be actual text in the cited transcript segments. Outcomes stay independent: inspection, treatment acceptance, agreement signature, payment, cancellation request/acceptance and retention save. Review decisions preserve the original assessment, require a reason and compare the active version before saving. Fictional sample grades are interface test fixtures, not evaluations of the client's calls.

Final information means offering future-service information: tell the customer to contact the company for future pest-control needs. Sales additionally includes referral-program information; General does not inherit that Sales-specific step. It is not collecting contact details or asserting that an account was updated. The initial expectation belongs to Validate, the solution expectation to Understand; Retention transition/research belong to Validate and its resolution expectation to Understand. IDs, order, counts and thresholds remain unchanged.

ASR quality is not proof of complete capture. Uploaded transcripts start unverified; legacy live absence also defaults unverified. Authorized source review can annotate roles and explicitly attest completeness/quality against privately prepared media, with immutable text/timing and server-owned audit. It cannot directly publish a new model result. A source revision change holds the old grade until owner re-analysis. Unverified quality or unresolved roles still matter; model semantic/applicability uncertainty remains even after source attestation. Samples attest only their fictional displayed text.

The first parsed model response is immutable `originalAnalysis`; latest raw output is `latestModelAnalysis`. Effective results use trusted roles and reliable persisted source context and carry `analysisSourceRevision`. Unsupported passes/absence-based misses/coaching remain unresolved or omitted. Unrelated employee IDs cannot authorize customer quotes. Four no-objection points need complete attributable evidence, both employee/customer speech and no unknown role. A checkpoint reason alone cannot establish attribution or clear source uncertainty. Correction evidence must be actual selected employee text; decisions record source revision and do not replay onto a newer model/source. These guards do not prove semantic rubric accuracy from a quote alone; actual model/audio evaluation remains pending.

The four fictional examples now use explicit checkpoint mappings and the same guard/scorer. General service is 11/12 Green with future-service information missed; one-time service acknowledges a recurring-plan objection and its actual omissions. Inspection/retention keep applicability uncertainty rather than forcing a flattering grade. These are curated text illustrations, never evaluations of the supplied client recordings.

“Follow-up agreed” requires an accepted follow-up with actual cited segment text. Re-service intent alone is “Re-service discussed”; promised, unknown or reported-completed follow-ups are not accepted commitments or verified CRM actions.
