# Scorecard policy

Sales contains the original 17 checkpoints. Gold: 17; Green: 14–16; Below: 0–13. General and Retention each contain their own 12 checkpoints. Gold: 12; Green: 11; Below: 0–10. Exact IDs, labels and guidance live in `src/lib/scoring/rubrics.ts`.

Passed earns one point; missed earns zero. A complete Sales call with reliably no objections earns exactly four objection-section policy points. Unknown, unverifiable or unresolved applicability does not change the denominator or become a pass. Incomplete calls or unresolved checkpoints receive no official grade. Missing/duplicate/wrong rubric checkpoints are rejected.

Evidence quotes must be actual text in the cited transcript segments. Outcomes stay independent: inspection, treatment acceptance, agreement signature, payment, cancellation request/acceptance and retention save. Review decisions preserve the original assessment, require a reason and compare the active version before saving. Fictional sample grades are interface test fixtures, not evaluations of the client's calls.
