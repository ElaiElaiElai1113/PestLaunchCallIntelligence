# Scorecard policy

Sales contains the original 17 checkpoints. Gold: 17; Green: 14–16; Below: 0–13. General and Retention each contain their own 12 checkpoints. Gold: 12; Green: 11; Below: 0–10. Exact IDs, labels and guidance live in `src/lib/scoring/rubrics.ts`.

Passed earns one point; missed earns zero. A complete Sales call with reliably no objections earns exactly four objection-section policy points. Unknown, unverifiable or unresolved applicability does not change the denominator or become a pass. Incomplete calls, unresolved checkpoints or any independent review reason withhold the official grade. Missing/duplicate/wrong rubric checkpoints are rejected.

Evidence quotes must be actual text in the cited transcript segments. Outcomes stay independent: inspection, treatment acceptance, agreement signature, payment, cancellation request/acceptance and retention save. Review decisions preserve the original assessment, require a reason and compare the active version before saving. Fictional sample grades are interface test fixtures, not evaluations of the client's calls.

Final information means offering future-service information: tell the customer to contact the company for future pest-control needs. Sales additionally includes referral-program information; General does not inherit that Sales-specific step. It is not collecting contact details or asserting that an account was updated. The initial expectation belongs to Validate, the solution expectation to Understand; Retention transition/research belong to Validate and its resolution expectation to Understand. IDs, order, counts and thresholds remain unchanged.

ASR quality is not proof of complete source capture. Ordinary uploaded transcripts persist `transcriptCompleteness: unverified` and independent `transcriptReviewReasons`; a legacy live record without the completeness field also defaults to unverified. These are server-owned fields with no public certification endpoint. Clearly fictional curated sample fixtures retain their known-complete test state.

The parsed model response is preserved separately as `originalAnalysis`. The effective assessment uses trusted segment speaker labels and the persisted completeness context. Unsupported employee passes, absence-based misses on an unverified transcript, and employee-specific coaching become unresolved or are omitted. Adding an unrelated employee segment cannot authorize a customer-only quote. Four no-objection policy points require complete attributable evidence with both employee and customer speech and no unknown speaker. A reviewer reason cannot establish source attribution or clear independent completeness/quality reasons. Trusted source completeness/attribution review remains a release prerequisite; these guards do not establish semantic rubric accuracy from a quote alone.

“Follow-up agreed” requires an accepted follow-up with actual cited segment text. Re-service intent alone is “Re-service discussed”; promised, unknown or reported-completed follow-ups are not accepted commitments or verified CRM actions.
