# Client V1 demonstration script

Target: 4–5 minutes. Show how a reviewer understands what happened, assesses employee handling and identifies what to practise next. Do not call this a completed client submission until the external checklist passes.

## Deployed recording demonstration — pending external acceptance

1. **0:00–0:25 — Purpose.** “This workspace brings the call outcome, transcript evidence, scorecard and coaching together. It separates customer commitments from signed agreements, payment and verified account actions.” Sign in with the authorized invited account. State the dataset and recording are fictional or verified privately sanitized.
2. **0:25–1:00 — New recording.** Upload a newly recorded, privately prepared fictional **spoken conversation**. Show genuine durable stages, not a sample result or a percentage. This segment is unavailable while Groq is empty and deployment/provider acceptance is pending. Silence or container bytes demonstrate admission only.
3. **1:00–1:45 — What happened.** Open Summary. Identify primary purpose and secondary intents, customer-reported need, important details and independently evidenced outcomes. For an inspection call, distinguish inspection booking from treatment acceptance, signature and payment. Show one meaningful ambiguity rather than forcing a conclusion.
4. **1:45–2:45 — Evidence and score.** Inspect timestamped transcript and authorized prepared-recording playback. Follow a checkpoint citation. Explain Sales 17 versus distinct General/Retention 12, with fixed denominators. If source attribution is unresolved, review each clear speaker, completeness and quality with a reason; leave uncertain segments Unknown. Show that this preserves original source/model history and withholds the old grade until owner re-analysis. Never claim automatic diarization or redaction.
5. **2:45–3:45 — Coaching and correction.** Show one employee-specific strength and improvement with a checkpoint, actual quote and useful suggested response. Make a supported reasoned correction using actual employee evidence. Show original/current decision history and the source revision. Explain why a checkpoint reason cannot certify an unverified transcript or imply a completed CRM action.
6. **3:45–4:30 — Review interface and handoff.** Return to the searchable/filterable call log and navigate within the filter. Briefly explain invite-only Supabase/private Storage, server-only Groq and ID-only Workflow boundaries. Close with limitations and remaining acceptance. Only demonstrate deletion with deliberately disposable fictional data. Customer end-of-test deletion happens after the full test, not during this recording.
